    Curbcut

    A free, self-hosted platform to find, fix and prove web accessibility.

    Curbcut scans whole websites against WCAG 2.2 AA with headless Chrome and axe-core, tracks every issue from the day it appears to the day it is fixed, and generates a public accessibility statement. I started it after the European Accessibility Act came into force, because the good tools are expensive.

    • Laravel
    • PHP
    • MySQL
    • axe-core
    • Headless Chrome
